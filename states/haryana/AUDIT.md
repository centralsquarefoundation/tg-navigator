## Math (all grades)
- Front: academic plan table (अकादमिक प्लान: month, chapter, competency code, competency),
  teaching-method tables, विद्या प्रवेश.
- Chapter opener page: title, illustration, box headed **"दक्षताएँ :"** (not "जानें" as the
  profile first assumed; transcribers recorded the printed heading).
- Lesson-plan pages: chapter band + "Lesson Plan · Class N"; 2–3 columns, each a unit with a yellow
  objectives box and a "Day a-b" badge; full-width bars "ब्लॉक N – name" and grey sub-step bars span
  all columns. A unit's blocks continue on the next page (badges repeated).
- Back: "0 कालांश" remedial plans, assessment skill passbooks, trackers (shown as original pages only).

## Hindi (G1–G3)
- Front: contents, NIPUN, competencies, block outline, materials, annual + weekly plan
  (weeks 11–12 and 20–21 are periodic assessment/review weeks; day 5 = weekly revision, day 6 =
  weekly assessment), textbook strategy.
- Day page: six "दिवस" tabs (current one in strong fill/dark text) + "सप्ताह-N" band; periods
  "1. … ⏱ 30 मिनट", steps "1.1 …"; G1/G2 also have a multigrade box ("बहुकक्षा शिक्षण हेतु पाठ योजना").
- Some days span two pages; the second page prints no tabs/band (joined automatically).
- Back: remedial plans, multigrade samples, trackers, print-rich, classroom management (original pages only).
